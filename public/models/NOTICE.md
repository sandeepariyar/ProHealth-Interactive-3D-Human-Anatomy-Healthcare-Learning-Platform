# Anatomical asset credits — ProHealth Next

## Data provenance and distribution

The real anatomical atlas used in this demonstration consists of the **seven aligned Z-Anatomy GLB systems** distributed by [nqwrc/3d-anatomy](https://github.com/nqwrc/3d-anatomy): skeletal, muscular, visceral, cardiovascular, nervous, joints, and lymphatic.

The anatomical mesh data is not owned by the author of ProHealth. The original repo states that the combined models are **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (noncommercial, attribution, share-alike). Model-derived assets and adaptations must retain applicable notices and terms. Read [the upstream LICENSE](https://github.com/nqwrc/3d-anatomy/blob/master/LICENSE), [NOTICE](https://github.com/nqwrc/3d-anatomy/blob/master/NOTICE) and [model Licence.txt](https://github.com/nqwrc/3d-anatomy/blob/master/public/models/License.txt) before reusing.

Required model attributions, reproduced from upstream:

>

Other contributing sources listed upstream include:

- Cranial Nerves and Foramina — University of Dundee, CAHID — CC BY 4.0.
- Anatomy of the Inner Ear — University of Dundee School of Medicine — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- Kidney — lissiecowley — CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Brainder and White matter — University of Washington; the upstream repo does not identify their specific licences.

The model files are **automatically downloaded upon npm install**, and served with the built demo. The Git repository tracks the download manifest and attribution. The browser also attempts a public hosted fallback. The original meshes are re-shaded and scaled together for demonstration; their anatomical positions are not reconstructed from a specific patient. The 3D viewer is a concept, not clinical software or a diagnostic device.

The application source code is distinct from the Creative Commons model data. The upstream share-alike rules may apply to distributed adaptations of the anatomical data. This is not legal advice.
